How to play Hong Kong Mahjong

Goal: be first to make a complete hand of 14 tiles: four sets plus one pair. A set is a pung (three alike), a kong (four alike) or a chow (three in a row in one suit).

Your turn: you draw a tile, then tap a tile to discard. When someone discards, you can claim it: Pung or Kong from anyone, Chow only from the player on your left, or Mahjong if it completes your hand. A claimed set is laid face up.

Flowers & seasons are set aside as they arrive and replaced. Your own flower and season (matching your seat wind) score a bonus.

Faan are points for how special your hand is. You need the table minimum to win. The bigger the faan, the more you collect: the player who discarded your winning tile pays it all; if you draw it yourself, everyone pays half.

Tip: with Ready hints on, tiles that would leave you one tile from winning get a gold dot, and the bar shows what you’re waiting on.
