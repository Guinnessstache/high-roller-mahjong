Realistic player characters: Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library
https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox

Models used: Business_Male_01 (m005), Business_Female_01 (f014), Business_Male_02 (m008),
Business_Female_02 (f015), Business_Male_03 (m020). Textures were resized and re-encoded
(JPEG/PNG) for the game; the meshes are unchanged.
