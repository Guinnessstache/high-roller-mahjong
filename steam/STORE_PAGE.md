## About This Game

**Mahjong night, without the drive.**

High Roller Mahjong puts you and three friends around a rosewood table in a high-end Macau casino. Everyone's webcam shows up right at their seat and their voice comes from across the table, so the groans when you claim their discard are real.

**Your table, your rules**
- Create a table and invite Steam friends in two clicks, or share a five-letter table code.
- You host the game yourself — no accounts, no subscriptions, no servers to go down. Steam connects your friends to you, anywhere in the world.
- Choose the minimum faan to win, the limit, the game length, flowers and seasons, and house rules like Seven Pairs.

**Real Hong Kong Mahjong**
- Pung, kong, chow and win claims with proper priority, robbing the kong, flowers and seasons, dealer repeats and wind rounds.
- Every win shows the hand, the faan breakdown and who pays what — the game does the counting.

**New to mahjong? It teaches you**
- A built-in guide to the rules and every scoring pattern.
- Ready hints mark which discard leaves you one tile from winning, and show what you're waiting on.

**Never short a player**
- *Play vs bots* seats you at a full table instantly.
- Easy, Medium and Hard computer players: from claim-happy beginners to sharp players who count tiles and play defense.
- Bots keep the table at four and hand their seat to a friend who joins.

**Tiles you can feel**
- Every tile is a real piece: watch it leave the wall, land in a hand and clack into the discards.
- Seated, overhead and free-camera views in a casino of red lanterns, neon and the clatter of other tables.

**Couch and handheld ready** — full controller support (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, Steam Deck) with an on-screen keyboard and a scalable interface for your TV.

**20 Steam achievements** — from your first win to Thirteen Orphans, robbing a kong, or beating three Hard bots.

*No real money. Points have no cash value and can't be bought.*
